## Research limits

- The existing FoR held-out test result is weak: accuracy `0.5542`, ROC-AUC `0.7349`, EER `0.3272`, and false-negative rate `0.8235`. Do not market this as a reliable authenticity verdict.
- Two isolated streaming candidates scored better on selected validation or reserved subsets but failed to generalize: the FoR candidate missed 36% of synthetic test clips, and the re-recording candidate missed 68% on the diagnostic `for_rerec` test split. Neither candidate is active.
- The ASVspoof metrics in the project are from balanced subsets; they are not full-set performance. See the saved report and dataset notes before quoting any number.
- Audio scores are estimates, not proof. Uncertain outcomes are valid. Performance on new generators, codecs, microphones, and noisy conditions is not established.
- Live monitoring analyzes a browser-provided microphone, shared-tab/system stream, or replay. It is not integrated with a phone network.

## Live stream stop-tail correction and FoR candidate retest — 2026-10-21

A stream must contain at least one hop before its first score; after full windows exist, the tail must contain at least half a hop of new audio beyond the overlap. An exact-window recording does not create a duplicate. Summary window end times are clamped to the real audio duration, and frames after finalization are rejected.
