Documented Model Limitations & Operational Scope

Forensic verification boundaries and operational disqualifications
dataset
Trained on public benchmark data

Model weights were trained primarily on FoR-2sec. In-the-wild synthesized speech, voice cloning zero-shot models (e.g. recent diffusion vocoders), and unseen codecs can reduce detection confidence.

hearing_disabled
Re-recording and room acoustic degradation

Microphone-to-speaker transfer functions (air acoustics) attenuate high-frequency phase cues, shifting probability scores toward the Uncertain band (0.40 – 0.60).

timelapse
Short utterance bias

Audio samples under 1.5 seconds contain fewer spectral window transitions, yielding higher variance in per-window classification.

gavel
Non-evidential output

Classification scores represent probabilistic acoustic anomaly vectors, not definitive legal or forensic proof of identity. Secondary spectral and bi-spectral audits are required for evidentiary submission.

science This is a research prototype. A result is an additional verification signal, not proof.
